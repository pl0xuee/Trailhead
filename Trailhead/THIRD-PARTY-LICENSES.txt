Trailhead's built-in places (Trailhead/Data/Seed.lua) are derived from the data
bundled with the MIT-licensed "Waypoint Tracker" addon, which in turn bundles data from
All The Things and pfQuest. Their licences are reproduced below, as the MIT licence requires.
Class and profession trainers (names, zones and map positions, which are facts about the game)
are taken from Warcraft Tavern's WoW Forever guides: https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/class-trainers/
and .../profession-trainers/. Weapon masters and riding trainers (Trailhead/Data/Known.lua) were checked
against .../weapon-trainers/ and .../riding/. The kinds of shop (general goods, food, reagents,
weapons, armor, ammunition, trade supplies) come from the Warcraft wiki's vendor categories
(https://wowpedia.fandom.com), used only as lists of names. Flight points are checked against the
game's own TaxiNodes table.
